The old man staggered along the worn path, his brittle bones creaking under the strain. He grasped an abandoned branch he had found along the side of the path in lieu of a cane, and, strangely, kept the other hand on the pommel of his sidesword—as if danger had never quite released him from its ensnaring grasp.

He staggered onwards until his foot seemed to catch on a rock seemingly laid for the purpose of catching up unsuspecting travelers. With a croaking gasp, the man fell forwards; only by sheer luck was he unharmed. The man, cursing all the while, decided to pull himself over to the side of the road, and take a break, perhaps finding a creek from which to drink and bathe.

Yes, he decided. A creek would be in quite good order. And perhaps a rabbit caught, and a fire going, and a stew simmering. And so the man summoned the larger portion of his flagging strength and stood up. He swayed like a willow tree flung waywards by the summer easterlies for a moment, then, in some burst of former glory, stood stock-still, hand on the worn pommel of his simple, leather-wrapped sword, stick laid forgotten on the ground. And if one squinted, they might be able to make out an afterimage, faint but glimmering, of a man with whipcorded muscle and sinew and a noble bearing about him, gripping the bejeweled pommel of a masterwrought sword in one hand and his silvered helm in the other.

Yet this image faded into the dust swirling along the road as a horse and cart turned the corner. The dust-browned mare trotted at a leisurely pace towards the old man, pulling its weathered cargo behind it.

“Hail,” said the cloaked individual atop the mare, giving the customary three-fingered greeting. The old man returned it, albeit much slower, and stepped aside to let the cart pass. Yet the horse simply stood there, the man atop it staring at him with an inscrutable gaze. The old man met his gaze with one made of steely iron, still blade-sharp. There was a glint in those gray-green orbs, a challenge for this man, this creature, if he would meet it.

The man did not.
