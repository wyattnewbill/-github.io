White Elephants

written by

Wyatt Newbill

INT. APARTMENT - NIGHT

A small, cluttered APARTMENT. PAN ACROSS the room and a life shared: a MOVIE PAUSED on the TV, the entryway cluttered with jackets and shoes, an UMBRELLA and PAIR OF HEELS visible - then: the KITCHEN. JOSH (23) and JADE (21) eat together at the TABLE.

Josh is dressed in sweats, and Jade is dressed for a night out. Her PURSE lays on the table next to a ceramic WHITE ELEPHANT. Two WINEGLASSES and a near-empty WINE BOTTLE are next to PLATES of dinner. Josh takes a sip of his wine - Jade's glass has water. They're in the middle of a conversation.

JOSH

Where is it?

JADE

You know - the Bowery.

JOSH

Oh. I - uh - that's nice.

Jade nods a little.

JOSH (CONT'D)

(suddenly)

Make sure to, y'know... take an umbrella - it might rain tonight.

JADE

Alright.

Josh sits at the table and absently twists a GOLD RING around his middle finger. He stares outside for a moment before an outburst escapes his lips.

JOSH

You sure you don't want me to come? I can get ready real quick - Y'know, I mean -

JADE

(cutting him off)

Josh, I told you I was going to this party a week ago.

JOSH

(a little quieter)

You don't even like these people.

JADE

Yeah, well, I wanna go anyways, OK?

She pushes her plate away, looking a little disgusted, and drains her glass in one gulp.

JOSH

(a little childlike)

Well, I think I should come -

JADE

No.

JOSH

You sure?

JADE

Yes.

Rain begins to patter on the window. Jade stands up, takes her plate to the SINK, and starts to wash it. Outside the window, cars honk and smoke trails from buildings.

JOSH

(watching her reflection in the window)

It's raining.

JADE

It is.

JOSH drains the rest of his wine before looking down at the ring on his finger.

JOSH

I still love you, y'know.

JADE

(softening a little)

I know.

JOSH

You should stay inside. Eat with me - we can finish watching the movie.

They both turn and look at the den, where the TV is paused.

JOSH (CONT'D)

I mean, I just think that maybe we should rest a little. You should take a break, y'know? It's better for... all of us.

JADE

(firmer)

I don't need to rest - I'm going to the party, Josh. Give it up.

JOSH

But it's not just about what you need anymore, Jade.

JADE

(looking right at him)

And it's not about what you need, either.

She moves to the table and grabs her purse, then walks over to the entryway. Josh pours the rest of the wine in his glass.

JADE (CONT'D)

I told you about this last week, and there was no problem then, was there? What's changed?

JOSH

I mean, come on, Jade - you know what's changed. We have to think about -

JADE

(cutting him off, clearly annoyed now)

Look, Josh, I'm gonna go have fun. My life isn't just going to stop, OK? That's not how this works.

She begins to check her makeup in the MIRROR by the entryway.

JOSH

(forcing it)

Well, you should eat something before you go. I'll heat up something - there's the leftovers from last night, and I know you've been craving pickle chips -

JADE

I'm not hungry, Josh.

JOSH

You were eating a ton of food earlier -

JADE

Well, I'm not hungry now.

Jade's PHONE pings all of a sudden, and she looks at it. Her expression changes for half a second before she stuffs it in her jacket. Josh notices and twists the ring on his finger.

JADE (CONT'D)

They're waiting for me.

(beat)

Look, I'm off, alright? I'll be back later, and we'll watch the movie then, I promise.

She turns towards the door and steps towards it, but Josh stands up suddenly, and the white elephant falls, smashing on the floor. They both startle, and Jade turns and rushes over.

JADE (CONT'D)

Are you alright?!

JOSH

Yeah... I'm fine.

(beat, to himself)

I'm fine.

Jade brings a DUSTPAN over wordlessly. They both pick up the pieces in tense silence. Josh stands up and throws them away, grabs a fresh BOTTLE from the CABINET - on which is a CALENDAR with a date circled in RED - and pours himself a glass. He turns to fill Jade's, but she puts a hand over it.

JOSH (CONT'D)

(laughing a little)

Right.

Jade puts her hand over his, covering the ring, and looks at him a little sadly.

JADE

I'm going, Josh. Give it up.

JOSH

You can't just keep doing this.

JADE

Doing what?

JOSH

Going out. Pretending that everything's -

He pauses.

JADE

Everything's what?

JOSH

(softly)

Fine.

The rain patters more intensely.

JOSH (CONT'D)

It's raining pretty hard, y'know.

JADE

(smiling)

I'll take an umbrella, Josh.

She pecks him on the cheek and goes to the door. He stares out the window while fiddling with his ring again.

JOSH

(not turning)

What - what am I supposed to do?

(beat)

You're the one who -

He can't bring himself to say it. Jade stops, facing the door.

JADE

I don't know.

They both stand there, the rain falling harder now.

JADE (CONT'D)

It's just a party, Josh.

JOSH

Nothing's just anything anymore, Jade. You know that.

Jade rests her hand on the KNOB, but doesn't open the door.

JOSH (CONT'D)

(broken)

Will - will he be there?

Jade pauses for a long moment. She doesn't respond.

JADE

Goodnight, Josh.

The door closes.

JOSH

(whispering)

I love you, y'know.

He stares out the window. The wineglasses still sit on the table, one empty, one half full.

CUT TO BLACK.
