Continuum

written by

Wyatt Newbill

A BLACK SCREEN. Darkness. Then a VOICE begins to speak to us.

NARRATOR

What is a dream?

NARRATOR (CONT'D)

Is it something that happens to us? (beat) Or something we choose?

A MONTAGE, only a SECOND. PURE SENSATION. A PUPIL DILATES, A RUSH OF WIND, AND A BREATH AS IF YOU HAVE JUST WOKEN UP.

CUT TO:

EXT. HILLSIDE - MIDDAY

Ash falls like snow. A TICKING can be heard.

Close on A WATCH on a BOY's wrist. It's scratched, scuffed, but stubbornly still ticking.

The BOY kneels, holding a GIRL. She's dead. Her brilliantly blue eyes are open - the only source of color in a world covered with ash.

NARRATOR

Is it an escape from reality? Is it where reality comes from? (beat) Or is it something stranger?

The boy closes her eyes, gently, lovingly. A tear falls from his face onto hers, tracing a line down her cheek. It's shared between them, a union made of sorrow.

He looks up. Right past us. The TICKING begins to speed up, crescendoing as he stares, eyes resolute. He begins to stand as the ticking climaxes.

CUT TO BLACK. TITLE CARD is shown.

CUT TO:

INT. CAR - NIGHT

Laughter and music. It's a different place and time.

The camera sits in the backseat, watching them laugh as music plays softly from the radio. The city lights smear across the windshield.

The GIRL leans her head out the window, wind in her hair. The BOY steals glances at her when he thinks she's not looking. They're in love.

CUT TO:

EXT. TRUCK - NIGHT

Lights cut through the darkness. A massive TRUCK thunders past, the words LUCID INC. barely visible on its side.

INT. TRUCK - CONTINUOUS

The DRIVER scrolls - one hand on the wheel and the other holding his phone. He laughs as his hand slackens on the wheel and the TRUCK starts to drift. He lifts his eyes and they widen.

CUT TO:

INT. CAR - NIGHT

Silence. Not awkward, but peaceful. The song ends, and another begins. The GIRL lights up.

GIRL

(excitedly)

Oh, I love this song!

She sings along, off-key and earnest. The boy looks at her for a second, looking at her the way every girl wants to be looked at.

Then - A BLINDING LIGHT washes over his face. His smile dies - he looks forward - his eyes widen - the camera turns -

TIME STOPS.

Everything frozen.

He looks desperately into the lens.

BOY

(pleading)

Save her...

The words ECHO, folding in on themselves and reverberating throughout dreamspace.

Time slams back into motion. A HORN blares.

CUT TO:

INT. APARTMENT - DAWN

The BOY jolts awake. He's a little older now, with an unkempt, scruffy beard. His eyes are hollow.

The faint echo of the crash can still be heard, ringing through the air. VANCE WHEELER shakes his head to dispel the last of the dream.

He sits upright, groaning, then scoots to the bed, resting his feet on the floor.

He's in an apartment with an entire wall of glass looking out on a semi-futuristic city. Skyscrapers line the horizon, and highways below that. It's all gray and white.

He reaches to the nightstand, taking a PILL and downing it with some water.

Close on the nightstand - bottles scattered: Antidepressants, DREAMS AWAY, DREAMLESS. And half-hidden behind the glass: the WATCH. Still ticking.

INT. KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Vance pours cereal. Eats mechanically. His apartment is simple - but there's no personality. Nothing to say VANCE lived there.

The TV blares.

NEWS ANCHOR

- Lucid Incorporated stocks continue to rise following the expansion of their Dream Management Program, now federally approved for trauma treatment -

Vance grabs the remote and turns it off. Silence.

CUT TO:

INT. LUCID INC. WAITING ROOM - DAY

The room is minimalist. Quiet. Magazines on a table, pictures on the wall. A WOMAN sits across from VANCE, trembling slightly.

WOMAN

First time too?

VANCE

(nods his head)

Feels like the opposite, though.

CUT TO:

INT. LUCID INC. DREAM CHAMBER - DAY

White walls, a soft hum emanating from what looks like a white sarcophagus in the center of the room. It's padded, lined with lights, sterile.

VANCE is inside it.

A TECHNICIAN (40s) sits next to the machine, attaching electrodes to his temples.

TECHNICIAN

You understood the rules and procedures?

He motions to a clipboard on a desk across the room, a signature visible on it.

VANCE

Yeah.

TECHNICIAN

Remember - you can dream anything you want.

Vance almost laughs.

VANCE

That's the problem.

CLOSE IN on VANCE's face.

TECHNICIAN (O.S.)

You'll feel a bit of a jolt.

Vance gasps, feeling like he's been punched in the chest.

SMASH CUT TO:

I/E. PERFECT LIFE - LATER

Sunlight filters through curtains. The GIRL lies beside VANCE, alive and breathing. They grow old together. We flash through vacations, laughter, life.

No tragedy. No watch. No ticking.

CUT TO:

INT. LUCID INC. DREAM CHAMBER - NIGHT

VANCE wakes smiling. At peace.

TECHNICIAN

How was it?

Vance considers.

VANCE

Peaceful... comfortable.

VANCE (CONT'D)

(pauses)

Too comfortable.

CUT TO:

INT. THRONE ROOM - DAY

Frantic, ratchety music starts to play as a WATCH ticks in the background.

VANCE sits on a throne. Adorned with gilded robes and a glittering crown.

SLOW DOLLY INTO VANCE, surrounded by finery and pretentiousness.

EXT. LUCID INC. WAITING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

VANCE sits alone. Hands clasped. Legs slowly beginning to bounce.

The camera mirrors the same DOLLY IN.

EXT. BATTLEFIELD - CONTINUOUS

VANCE - now a SOLDIER - is crawling through a battlefield as explosions roar and smoke clouds the camera. The sounds of battle assault his ears.

Keep DOLLYING IN through the noise, slowly locking onto his eyes.

INT. LUCID INC. WAITING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Closer now.

His fingers tap against his knees.

INT. LAB - CONTINUOUS

VANCE - a SCIENTIST - watches a breakthrough on a monitor.

Relief on his face.

The camera continues its steady push inward.

INT. LUCID INC. WAITING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Even closer.

Vance rubs his hands together, foot tapping a furious rhythm out on the floor.

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

VANCE - an ARTIST - paints furiously, eyes slightly crazed. His brush freezes mid-stroke, and his brow furrows.

INT. LUCID INC. WAITING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

The camera is uncomfortably close now. Vance's feet, out of frame now, are still tapping that frantic beat. He swallows. The room seems to hum.

EXT. DREAMSPACE - CONTINUOUS

VANCE stands alone. No costume. No role. No landscape. Just him and the void behind him. The camera completes the DOLLY IN, suspended right in his face. The music crescendos as... He blinks.

CUT TO:

INT. LUCID INC. WAITING ROOM - DAY

The music stops. The same waiting room. Same framing as before. But now Vance seems drained of the nervous energy that propelled him before.

The WOMAN from before glances at him across the room, uneasy.

CUT TO:

INT. LUCID INC. DREAM CHAMBER - DAY

The room is white, but not clinical. Still that soft light and low hum. VANCE lays back in the machine.

The TECHNICIAN checks a tablet, calm, practiced. This isn't their first conversation.

A pause. He doesn't start right away.

TECHNICIAN

Before we begin... I have to ask.

Vance looks at him and sighs.

VANCE

You're going to anyway.

TECHNICIAN

Most people come in knowing exactly what they want.

He gestures to the chair.

TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)

A place. A person. A moment they wish they could go back to.

The technician begins attaching sensors to Vance's temples.

TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)

We can do that.

Vance watches the ceiling.

VANCE

And after that?

The technician hesitates - slightly, but still noticeable.

TECHNICIAN

After that... people usually ask for more of the same.

The last sensor clicks into place.

TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)

Longer versions. Better outcomes. Lives without the parts that hurt.

VANCE

(exhales)

Does it work?

TECHNICIAN

(considering)

It works as intended.

VANCE

Which means?

The technician lowers his tablet and meets Vance's eyes.

TECHNICIAN

It gives you control.

A beat. The word hangs there, suspended in the air.

VANCE

And if I don't want that?

The technician raises an eybrow.

TECHNICIAN

Most people do.

VANCE

I know.

The technician pauses. Sets his tablet aside. This isn't in the script.

TECHNICIAN

Hypothetically -

Vance smiles faintly. He has him.

TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)

- if you could dream anything...

He taps the side of the chair nervously.

TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)

(subtle emphasis)

Any life. Any version of yourself. Any length of time. What would you choose?

Vance doesn't answer right away. His fingers curl slightly.

VANCE

I'd start small. Fix one moment. (beat) Then I'd stretch it.

The technician watches him closely now.

VANCE (CONT'D)

Make it safe. Predictable. (beat) And eventually... boring.

The technician smiles despite himself.

TECHNICIAN

That's usually the point where people stop.

VANCE

Because there's nothing left to want.

The technician doesn't disagree.

VANCE (CONT'D)

So then you'd want a surprise. Something you didn't plan.

The technician's smile fades slightly.

TECHNICIAN

That's where we recommend caution.

VANCE

Why?

TECHNICIAN

Because unpredictability means risk.

VANCE

(smiles)

So does being alive.

The technician studies him for a long moment. Then -

TECHNICIAN

If you keep going... keep pushing...

He gestures vaguely.

TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)

You'll eventually end up somewhere you didn't design and don't want.

VANCE

Where?

The technician hesitates. This is the closest he'll come to saying it.

TECHNICIAN

Somewhere... familiar.

VANCE

(beat)

And if I don't like it?

The technician reaches for the tablet.

TECHNICIAN

Then you'll wake up.

He stops. Looks back at Vance.

TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)

Or think you did.

The machine's HUM deepens. The lights flicker slightly.

CLOSE IN on Vance's face as he closes his eyes.

TECHNICIAN (O.S.)

Last chance.

VANCE

Take me all the way.

The hum swells.

CUT TO:

INT. APARTMENT - NIGHT

VANCE sits alone on the edge of his bed. His hands fidget and find their way, subconsciously, to a WATCH on the nightstand - the WATCH. His fingers trace its edge.

He picks it up. It ticks softly. He hesitates - then straps it to his wrist.

CUT TO:

EXT. CITY STREET - NIGHT

VANCE walks alone. Neon reflections glimmer off wet asphalt.

The WATCH ticks against his wrist, louder now.

A MAN jostles past him in a hurry. Vance stops. Looks around. Nothing is wrong. Everything is familiar.

A small smile flickers - then disappears. A little too familiar...

CUT TO:

INT. CAR - NIGHT

VANCE grips the wheel as the GIRL sits beside him, humming along to the radio. The song ends and she laughs softly.

He glances at her, then, slowly, to the WATCH on his wrist. The ticking is louder now, almost echoing in the enclosed space.

VANCE

Can you hear that?

GIRL

(shouting, giggling)

Hear what?

Horns. A blaring light. Shock. We've been here before.

CUT TO:

EXT. HILLSIDE - MIDDAY

Ash falls around him. He kneels, holding the GIRL's body.

Everything, color, and sound, is muted except for the bright blue of her EYES and the TICKING of the WATCH.

VANCE's hands tremble. He slowly closes the GIRL's eyes.

The WATCH TICKS faster.

Vance lifts his face towards the gray sky as something inside him shifts.

VANCE

(whispers)

I've... been here before.

CUT TO:

INT. LUCID INC. DREAM CHAMBER - NIGHT

Red LIGHTS start to flare as MONITORS beep and chime. The TECHNICIAN whirls around from his seat at the desk.

TECHNICIAN

Shit!

CUT TO:

INT. APARTMENT - NIGHT

Back at the bed.

VANCE is still toying with the watch, but now the glass, rather than showing the city, flickers between the city street, car, hillside - fragments of his life reflected in the window like a slideshow.

The WATCH's ticking starts to slow.

VANCE sits quietly. He traces the edge of the watch again, then closes his eyes.

VANCE

I've always been here.

He smiles, a small, almost imperceptible smile.

CUT TO:

INT. LUCID INC. DREAM CHAMBER - NIGHT

The MONITORS still beep. The TECHNICIAN frantically loads some liquid into a syringe and plunges it into his arm.

He worriedly watches VANCE's face for a second. Nothing.

TECHNICIAN

Shit!

He presses a few buttons on his tablet and takes a breath.

Then rips the electrodes off VANCE's temples.

SMASH CUT TO:

EXT. DREAMSPACE

It's a void. No ground. No sky. No horizon. No sound. Just VANCE, suspended in nothing.

He breathes in and out, eyes slowly opening.

In front of him, the GIRL appears. Finally present. She gazes at him with familiarity - love.

GIRL

You made it.

VANCE

I didn't make it. (beat) I came back.

He laughs softly, cynically.

VANCE (CONT'D)

I thought I was running from this.

She steps closer.

GIRL

You weren't running.

VANCE

Then what was I doing?

GIRL

(considering)

You were playing.

VANCE

Playing what?

She doesn't answer. Instead, she reaches out and gently places the WATCH into his hand. It's ticking.

GIRL

You wanted something that could surprise you.

The ticking grows louder.

GIRL (CONT'D)

Something you couldn't control.

His brow furrows as the NARRATOR's voice returns - now unmistakably VANCE's.

NARRATOR (V.O.)

If you could dream any dream you wanted... you'd start with pleasure. (beat) Then curiosity. (beat) Then the gamble. The unknown. (beat) The one thing you can't escape.

The void begins to FADE, replaced by rapid-fire flashes of all the dreams and lives VANCE has lived. Everything he both is and isn't. Until the last one -

SMASH CUT TO:

EXT. HILLSIDE - MIDDAY

We are back where we began.

The BOY kneels, holding the GIRL'S BODY. Ash falls. The world is BLACK AND WHITE. The WATCH is ticking.

But now - the BOY doesn't stand. Doesn't look for an answer.

He sits. Breathes. Comes to terms with it - feels its full weight.

GIRL (V.O.)

(soft)

You are not a stranger here. (beat) You are what happens when the universe chooses to feel something.

The ticking slows.

CLOSE IN on the BOY's face. His eyes lift, not in defiance or despair, but in recognition. The camera flashes to his view. It's the girl in the distance.

GIRL (V.O.) (CONT'D)

Go live.

A breath. Then one final whisper.

GIRL (V.O.) (CONT'D)

Wake up.

CUT TO BLACK.

A sharp gasp of air - from the montage at the beginning. Like someone surfacing from deep water. We hear BREATHING - fast at first, then slowing.

One final sound:

The single TICK of a WATCH.

Then - NOTHING.
