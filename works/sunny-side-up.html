Sunny Side Up

written by

Wyatt Newbill

I/E. DINER - NIGHT

It's night in the city. Trains rumble overhead as cars honk and the haze of fluorescence replaces what was the glare of daylight.

We're outside a DINER: Sunny Side Up. The neon "Open 24 Hours" SIGN in the window buzzes faintly. The BELL rings as a customer, JOE (40s), walks inside.

WILL (19), the waiter, looks up from behind the COUNTER and greets him warmly. He's a regular. In the corner booth, OLIVIA (8), looks up hopefully - but her gaze drops back to her half-finished PLATE of eggs as she spies the customer.

WILL

Hey, how ya doin?

JOE

Good, good! How's school comin'?

WILL

Well, it's comin'.

They both laugh a little, but Joe sobers a little as he glances at the corner booth.

JOE

Aw, someone beat me to it.

Will glances over. A pause as they both look at the corner booth and the little kid inside.

WILL

Yeah.

(beat)

Mind takin' the counter tonight?

JOE

Yeah, sure.

(glances over again)

Guess she's got good taste.

He begins to head over to the counter.

WILL

The regular?

JOE

The regular.

Will goes out to the kitchen doors.

WILL

(shouting)

Joe's here! The regular, Table 6!

He walks back behind the counter and starts to fill SYRUP BOTTLES for the next day. Just then, the doors open, and the MANAGER (40s) walks out carrying a CLIPBOARD.

MANAGER

Will!

(beat as Will looks up and nods)

Everything good out here?

WILL

As usual.

The manager watches the room for a moment until his gaze lands on the corner booth. His brow furrows a little.

MANAGER

She still here?

WILL

Yeah.

MANAGER

Since when?

Will pauses.

WILL

I... don't know.

(beat)

Karen's shift, maybe?

The manager frowns. Across the diner, Olivia shifts eggs around her plate absentmindedly.

MANAGER

Parents in the restroom, or...?

WILL

Haven't seen anybody.

A beat. Across the diner, the bell rings as the door opens and a customer walks in. Olivia looks up nervously, hopefully, but her face falls. She goes back to staring at her food.

MANAGER

Go check on her. I'll take care of this guy.

The manager walks over to the customer and begins conversing with him. Will slowly begins to walk over to Olivia. He stops beside the booth.

WILL

Hey.

Olivia looks up. She's a little disheveled. A LARGE BACKPACK is tucked tightly against her side, like she's afraid someone might take it. The backpack is old and worn, but loved.

WILL (CONT'D)

(gesturing to the eggs)

You still workin' on those?

She looks down at her plate.

OLIVIA

I'm savin' em'.

WILL

Savin' em'?

OLIVIA

(nodding)

For later.

Will smiles politely, not thinking much of it.

WILL

Need anything else?

Olivia pauses for a bit, thinking hard.

OLIVIA

What time do d - d -

WILL

(gently)

- Diners -

OLIVIA

(slowly, sounding it out)

Diners... close?

WILL

This one doesn't.

She nods sagely, digesting it as if it's useful information.

WILL (CONT'D)

You waiting on someone?

OLIVIA

(looking around a little)

My mom.

WILL

She step out?

OLIVIA

She told me she'd... be back.

A pause. Will nods to himself a little and begins to turn.

OLIVIA (CONT'D)

...Can I stay until she gets back?

Will turns back. Her legs swing above the floor. He looks around the nearly empty diner and sees that the manager's taken care of the customer and gone back inside.

WILL

(slowly, smiling gently)

...Sure - sure.

(beat)

Mind if I sit?

Olivia nods, and Will slides into the booth across from her. There's a bit of an awkward silence as they stare at each other silently. Olivia breaks it first.

OLIVIA

What's your name?

WILL

Will.

She extends a hand with childish confidence. Will shakes it.

OLIVIA

I'm Olivia.

(beat)

You're supposed to say 'nice to meet you,' y'know.

WILL

Oh, sorry - nice to meet you, I guess.

Olivia nods seriously. A beat.

OLIVIA

Do you ever sleep?

Will blinks a little.

WILL

...Uh, yeah. Why?

OLIVIA

Well, you work at night, right?

WILL

...Yeah -

OLIVIA

So then you don't sleep, right?

WILL

I do, eventually.

OLIVIA

Oh.

(beat as she thinks a bit)

So you're like a bat, then?

WILL

(smiling a little)

Basically.

OLIVIA

Cool!

There's a beat of silence. Olivia looks back down at her plate, but then she perks up again as a new question comes to her. She leans in towards Will conspiratorially.

OLIVIA (CONT'D)

Do people here really eat pancakes at midnight?

WILL

(leaning in too)

Yeah... See the guy over there?

He points at Joe.

WILL (CONT'D)

Every night he comes in here and orders pancakes, eggs, and bacon.

Olivia stares at Joe in wonder and astonishment before she swivels back to Will.

OLIVIA

Does he really eat all of it?

WILL

Every last bite.

OLIVIA

(almost under her breath)

Woah...

Will starts a bit as he realizes he should be getting back to his job.

WILL

Speaking of which... his food should be ready by now.

He stands.

WILL (CONT'D)

Don't go anywhere.

Will smiles a bit as he realizes what he just said, but Olivia just nods with a soldier's countenance.

Will goes into the kitchen to bring Joe his pancakes. As he does, Olivia begins to take out the SUGAR PACKETS and stack them on the table.

Will comes back out and delivers Joe his plate. Joe sighs happily, ready to dig in, but -

JOE

Hey, Will - ain't ya forgettin' something?

WILL

Oh, right! Sorry 'bout that.

He quickly tops off one of the syrup bottles from earlier, then hurries over to Joe and hands him the syrup.

JOE

Thanks.

(as he's putting syrup on the pancakes)

She's waitin' on somebody?

Will's a little confused for a moment, before Joe motions to Olivia.

WILL

Oh, yeah.

JOE

Hope they don't keep her waiting too long, huh?

He turns back to the pancakes. Will, though, looks over at Olivia.

WILL

...Yeah.

He walks back over to Olivia and plops down in the booth. Olivia has tried, unsuccessfully, to make a tower of sugar packets.

OLIVIA

Why do you fill them before they're empty?

Will stares at her blankly.

OLIVIA (CONT'D)

The syrup.

WILL

(realizing, then shrugging)

So nobody runs out tomorrow.

Olivia nods, absorbing this new way of thinking.

OLIVIA

My mom always waits till we run out.

Just then, the bell rings. Olivia looks up quickly - someone's just walked out. She lowers her head, disappointed. Will watches in silence, before something occurs to him.

WILL

...How long have you been waiting for your mom?

Olivia thinks for a moment.

OLIVIA

Since... since the sun was out.

WILL

She must've called you, right?

OLIVIA

We don't have phones.

WILL

Do - do you have any family nearby?

OLIVIA

My grandma.

Will has a small moment of hope.

WILL

Can we call her?

Olivia shakes her head.

OLIVIA

She doesn't have a phone.

WILL

(beat)

Do you know where she lives?

Olivia thinks, then shakes her head. Nothing.

OLIVIA

My mom just talks about her a lot. I don't think we visited her since... I don't really remember.

Will swallows.

WILL

How - what did your mom say to you when she - dropped you off?

OLIVIA

She just told me to wait for her. She was gonna get our stuff, because it's moving day.

(beat)

Do... do you think she got lost?

Will's face falls - but Olivia recognizes nothing as wrong, and Will is going to try desperately to keep it that way.

WILL

Maybe. Grownups get lost all the time, y'know - I get lost a lot.

(beat)

Where are you guys moving?

OLIVIA

I don't know.

WILL

You don't know?

OLIVIA

Mom said it was somewhere better.

Will gazes at Olivia's backpack. It's slightly open, with old clothes sticking out. There are some sugar packets in the side pocket. He looks at her untouched eggs.

Will suddenly stands.

WILL

You ever had pancakes at midnight?

Olivia shakes her head.

WILL (CONT'D)

Then I think tonight's the night.

Without waiting for an answer, he heads towards the counter. Olivia follows, grabbing her backpack too. She sits down a seat away from Joe, who smiles at her invitingly.

Will goes into the kitchen to whip up some pancakes. Olivia waits at the counter, looking at Joe as he eats.

OLIVIA

Why do you always get pancakes?

Joe looks at her. There's a pause as he searches his memories.

JOE

It was - what was it - high school -

(he smiles as he remembers)

- high school graduation. My friend Jim swore these were the best pancakes in the city.

OLIVIA

Was he right?

JOE

Been trying to prove him wrong ever since.

He cuts into a pancake.

OLIVIA

Well, did you?

JOE

(smiling fondly)

Nope.

The door squeaks as Will comes out with a PLATE of two pancakes.

WILL

Here ya go - freshly-made pancakes. Dig in.

Olivia accepts the plate and takes a bite. Her eyes widen, and she pauses. She takes another. And another. And another.

JOE

(laughing a little)

Woah there, cowboy!

But Will just watches. He smiles faintly, then turns back towards the kitchen.

By the time Olivia's finished, Will's back with another plate. She takes it wordlessly. Joe, on the other hand, is finished.

JOE (CONT'D)

Hey, ring me up?

He hands him a 20.

WILL

Yeah.

As he begins to open the register, Joe holds out a hand and stops him.

JOE

No change today.

(beat)

Must be something in the air, huh?

(walking out and muttering to himself, smiling)

Guess she's got good taste after all.

Will smiles as he watches him leave, then puts the 20 in the register. He turns back towards Olivia, who's finished her second plate. She holds it out towards him, and he takes it, putting it behind the counter.

WILL

Wanna help me wipe the counters down?

Olivia nods excitedly. Will smiles fondly. He takes two towels from behind the counter and two spray bottles.

WILL (CONT'D)

Here, you take the stool tops and I'll take the counter.

He walks around and shows her how to do the stool tops. Olivia nods, and they wipe the counter and stool off together. She takes to the task with incredible seriousness.

Once they're done, Will goes to the wall and sits down heavily, waiting for the counter to dry. Olivia sits by him.

There's nobody in the diner but for one patron at a booth. Olivia looks at Will.

OLIVIA

Does everyone leave when a d - d -

WILL

- Diner -

OLIVIA

(slowly, sounding it out)

Diner... closes?

WILL

Yeah.

OLIVIA

But it opens again.

WILL

(beat)

Yeah.

She nods, and a gentle silence falls over the two of them.

OLIVIA

She's probably looking for us. Shouldn't we go outside?

Will looks at Olivia, a little heartbroken. He can't bring himself to answer her fully.

WILL

Where are you staying tonight?

OLIVIA

(like it's obvious)

Here.

WILL

No, I mean after here.

Olivia looks a little confused. She begins to lean on Will, exhausted.

OLIVIA

I don't know.

Eventually, Olivia falls asleep on Will's shoulder. Will gazes at the child tenderly for a moment, then swallows.

He takes out his PHONE and scrolls to a CONTACT. He hesitates for a second. He looks at Olivia, then presses call.

WILL

(a little forlorn)

Hey, Bill -

(beat)

Yeah, yeah, I'm doing alright. Making ends meet, y'know?

(beat)

Yeah, I'll tell them you said that.

(Will looks down at Olivia)

Look, the reason I called is because I... I got a kid here.

(beat)

I dunno. She just... showed up.

(beat)

Her mom -

He breaks off. He can't bring himself to say it.

WILL (CONT'D)

(beat)

- Yeah. Yeah.

(beat)

Yeah, I'm pretty sure. She, uh... she doesn't know where she's sleeping tonight.

(long beat)

No, Bill, I won't just leave her. She -

(softly)

- I know what that feels like, y'know?

(beat)

Yeah. Yeah. I know the drill. I'm here all night, anyways.

(beat)

Ok.

(beat)

Ok. Bye.

There's a beat, and then he hangs up. It's now that we see the contact: Bill (Social Worker). A small breath leaves him. Almost a laugh. Almost a cry.

He looks down at Olivia, asleep against his shoulder. He looks away, swallowing hard. The diner hums around them. But it's just Will, and the kid, sitting against the wall of a run-down diner. All alone, except for each other.

CUT TO BLACK.
