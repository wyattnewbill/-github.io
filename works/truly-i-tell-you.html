Truly I Tell You

written by

Wyatt Newbill

"If there were water
And no rock
If there were rock
And also water
And water
A spring
A pool among the rock"
T.S. Eliot, The Waste Land

FADE IN:

I/E. APARTMENT COMPLEX - DAY

Still shots from an upwards perspective: A packed CHURCH in the middle of the day, CROSS barely visible. An OLD MAN, sitting in a LOUNGE CHAIR on the BALCONY of his apartment, smoking a CIGARETTE. The ROOFS of buildings, trailing SMOKE. And finally, an immaculate, gleaming turquoise POOL in the middle of a NICE APARTMENT COMPLEX, seen as if from across the street.

Hold on the pool and SUPERIMPOSE TITLE.

It is now that faint voices can be heard. They crescendo in volume and anger as the conversation turns into an argument.

CUT TO:

INT. APARTMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

NICO (12) stares out the WINDOW of his APARTMENT ROOM - and it is now that we realize that the still shots from the beginning are all views from his room.

An old FAN whirs noisily in the background, but doesn't do much to help - sweat trickles down Nico's neck. The apartment is cramped and dirty, walls yellowed and peeling. They're obviously living in poverty.

His parents, THOMAS (late 20s) and EVE (late 20s) are the source of the voices arguing outside.

THOMAS

Jesus fucking christ, Eve! Do you not hear what the fuck you're talking about?

EVE

Well, guess what, Thomas! That is what the fuck I'm talking about!

(beat)

YES, I'm not happy with our lives! You think working as a receptionist is the big dream I had as a kid? You think that living in a shitty apartment in a shitty neighborhood is gonna make me happy?

THOMAS

You didn't think it was gonna be sunshine and rainbows when you married me, did you? I'm not your rich daddy, and I'm not gonna spoil you rotten while you go out and waste the years away!

Nico stares outwards at the church, body leaning towards the window slightly. He gazes at the preacher, who gesticulates wildly, entrancing his audience. Nico's parents fade into the background slightly, while the strains of the preacher gently floats up to Nico.

PREACHER

... And I tell you, tell you this from the bottom of my heart - you will witness the mercy of Christ, and be reborn...

But the preacher fades into the background as Thomas and Eve reassert themselves in Nico's mind with an outburst of noise. Nico starts a little, coming back to reality. He seems to be counting down in his head.

THOMAS

FUUUUUUUCK!

(beat, then somewhat more softly)

Is that what you want? That's all you want?

(beat)

You knew what you were getting into when you married me, Eve.

EVE

I knew who you were. I just - I didn't know we would still be here.

Nico pulls himself away from the window and goes to his door, peeking outside. It is now that we see Eve and Thomas for the first time.

Thomas stands in the kitchen, arms folded. Eve sits at the table, head in her hands. Nico looks back outside.

And turns the knob, opening the door and walking slowly outside. His parents start a little as they see him. They exchange glances, embarrassed.

THOMAS

Hey, buddy -

EVE

(jumping in)

Did you wake up from your nap?

A silence. Nico looks at Eve, then Thomas.

NICO

I - I'm going outside.

CUT TO:

EXT. APARTMENT COMPLEX - MOMENTS LATER

Nico is sitting in the empty POOL at his apartment complex. He lays on his back and watches the clouds as they pass overhead.

The pool is dirty, grimy - a cracked basin of faded blue tiles. They shimmer in the afternoon light, painting Nico's body in shades of aquamarine.

The CHURCH BELL rings. Once. Twice. Thrice. Nico sits up and looks out of the pool towards the church, bell tower poking up through the apartment buildings.

CUT TO:

EXT. CHURCH - MOMENTS LATER

Nico walks along the SIDEWALK by the CHURCH, creeping a little - as if he's intruding on something. The church doors loom in front of him, and he stares, entranced, at them.

Shouting CHILDREN interrupt his reverie, and he whips around - across the street, children play in the pool from earlier while their parents converse or lounge happily. It's a paragon of happiness and contentment.

Nico stares for a moment, before the voice of the preacher drifts out to him again.

PREACHER

...washed away, and made anew...

He turns back to the church doors, and begins to creep alongside the side of the church, ducking behind BUSHES and TREES.

He comes to a halt at a WINDOW, peering into the church. Through it, he sees a BAPTISM that is being performed - on a WOMAN with a passing resemblance to Eve. She kneels before a FONT by the preacher.

PREACHER (CONT'D)

Do you renounce the darkness of this world, and embrace His light?

WOMAN

I do.

PREACHER

Do you believe in God, the Father Almighty, creator of heaven and earth?

WOMAN

I do.

PREACHER

Do you believe in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord?

WOMAN

I do.

PREACHER

Do you believe in the Holy Spirit, and in the forgiveness of sins and the life everlasting?

WOMAN

I do.

The preacher takes water into his hand from the font, and the woman kneels over it.

PREACHER

Then, I baptize you in the name of the Father -

Water streams over her head.

PREACHER (CONT'D)

- and of the Son -

More water.

PREACHER (CONT'D)

- and of the Holy Spirit.

Water trickles down her face. There is silence. The preacher extends his hand.

PREACHER (CONT'D)

Rise.

She clasps his hand in the manner of Adam and God, and he lifts her to her feet.

PREACHER (CONT'D)

From the most holy water you return made anew. Welcome, sister, into the body of Christ.

Applause erupts from the AUDIENCE gathered, and a smile breaks out across the woman's face. Her FAMILY comes up to her, and they all hug each other.

All the while, Nico stares, entranced, at the scene.

SMASH CUT TO:

INT. APARTMENT - EVENING

Nico stares, but this time it's down at his PLATE. It's dinner, and the family is eating together. There's nothing but the clink of SILVERWARE against PLATES for a bit.

THOMAS

Nico, how was your day?

NICO

It was... good.

THOMAS

What'd you do?

NICO

Nothing.

(beat, then suddenly)

Do you think people can change?

He looks up. Eve and Thomas exchange a glance, and Eve smiles.

EVE

Of course they can, sweetie.

NICO

Really?

THOMAS

Yeah. People change all the time.

NICO

... How?

THOMAS

(shrugging)

You grow up. Things happen.

NICO

Do people always want to change?

EVE

... Sometimes they don't. And sometimes they do. But most of the time, it's for the better.

NICO

Okay.

They continue eating, this time Eve staring at her plate in silence. For a moment, everything is quiet. Then, in the distance, the church bell begins to ring. On the third ring, Thomas stands up.

THOMAS

I'm done.

He takes his plate and goes to the SINK. In the distance, the bell continues to ring - nine times in total.

CUT TO:

EXT. CHURCH - NEXT DAY

Nico stands in front of the church again. Today, the DOORS are open a crack, and the words of the preacher waft out with a little more force:

PREACHER

Amen, amen...

Nico creeps closer to hear more. A beat. He glances across the street - the pool is empty, no children playing or adults lounging.

He slips inside the church.

CUT TO:

INT. CHURCH - CONTINUOUS

As Nico enters, the preacher is thunderously sermonizing, using his hands wildly. The AUDIENCE is, as we saw at the beginning, entranced.

PREACHER

And so Nicodemus came to Jesus, under the cover of night, carrying all that he had been taught.

(beat)

PREACHER (CONT'D)

And Jesus said to him, "Very truly I tell you, no one can see the kingdom of God unless they are born again."

(beat, then, growing in volume)

For the Kingdom of Heaven is not entered by the old self. The Kingdom is perfect, and nothing imperfect can remain within it. The things we carry here - our failures, our fears, our sins — cannot follow us into the presence of God.

(beat, now wild with passion)

Call to Him with love, and you will be but newly baptized; Henceforth you never shall be the sinner - Henceforth you shall be accepted into the Kingdom of Heaven with loving, open arms - those arms of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ!

The audience breathes outwards as one, and, with one breath:

AUDIENCE

Amen.

The preacher moves towards the font in front of him, and reaches a hand towards the water.

PREACHER

Yet Nicodemus doubted the word of the Savior.

PREACHER (CONT'D)

And Jesus was merciful, and said, “Very truly I tell you, no one can enter the kingdom of God unless they are born of water and the Spirit. Flesh gives birth to flesh, but the Spirit gives birth to spirit."

(beat, placing his hand upon the water)

You who turns the wheel of your life and looks windward, look astern, look to shore, look to your Savior! Meet Him on the shores of salvation, meet Him in the Kingdom of Heaven, your sins washed away by the flood!

The preacher thrusts his arms outwards, silhouetted against the golden light streaming through the WINDOW behind him - looking in that moment for all the world like Jesus on the cross.

PREACHER (CONT'D)

(crescendoing, exclaiming)

You will enter the Kingdom of Heaven - sinner, ingrate, one who doubts in His word! You will repent, you will be bathed in His waters, - you will be reborn!

(beat)

YOU WILL BE SAVED, AND BY BEING REBORN WILL BE SAVED!

Nico is stunned, shocked by this revelation. The audience looks at the preacher in awe, and with one breath again:

AUDIENCE

Amen.

Nico gazes upwards in rapture.

CUT TO:

EXT. OUTSIDE APARTMENT - MOMENTS LATER

Nico has run back to the APARTMENT to share with his parents the news. But as he opens the DOOR a crack - a CRASH from inside. His mother has dropped a GLASS. Thomas comes running into the room.

THOMAS

What happened!

(seeing the glass)

Jesus, Eve -

EVE

I'm fine.

THOMAS

You're always fucking -

He stops himself. A beat.

EVE

What?

Thomas says nothing, and begins bending down to pick up pieces of glass.

EVE (CONT'D)

Thomas -

THOMAS

What?

EVE

... Nothing.

She kneels beside him. For a moment, they silently pick up the glass together as Nico watches.

Eve cuts her finger. A bead of blood wells up, and Thomas reaches for her hand.

THOMAS

(gruffly, tenderly)

You cut yourself.

EVE

It's fine.

THOMAS

(reaching for her hand)

Here, let me see.

For just a moment, they look at each other.

Then Eve pulls her hand away.

EVE

I'm fine.

The church bell begins to ring again:

Once. Nico looks at his parents.

Twice. They continue cleaning the glass.

Thrice. Nico looks towards the window, remembering the woman beneath the water.

Four.

PREACHER (V.O.)

(faintly)

You will be reborn...

Five.

PREACHER (V.O.) (CONT'D)

You will be saved...

Six. Nico's eyes settle on the broken glass.

Seven. He slowly steps backwards.

Eight. He turns. And walks away.

Nine. The doorway is empty, the bell done tolling.

CUT TO:

EXT. POOL - MOMENTS LATER

It's silent, the GATE closed, pool empty. The floors are white and immaculate, the pool perfectly turquoise, a flat sheet of glass. It's perfect.

Nico clambers up the gate and shimmies down the other side, reaching the ground. He approaches the pool slowly, almost as if in a trance. It shimmers invitingly.

NICO

(whispering)

I do.

He kicks off his shoes, stepping slowly into the water.

NICO (CONT'D)

(whispering)

I do.

He begins to walk out into the middle of the pool, his head getting closer to the water with every breath.

NICO (CONT'D)

(whispering)

I do.

His chin is now on the water, breath coming in gasps. The water ripples excitedly.

NICO (CONT'D)

(whispering)

I do...

And with a sudden, violent motion, he thrusts his head underwater. He gasps, and bubbles float up as water rushes into his mouth.

He fights towards the surface, and water splashes as he emerges, spluttering and couching. It didn't work. He gasps and breathes heavily.

NICO (CONT'D)

(determinedly)

... I will be saved...

He gazes up at the apartments that can be seen, vaguely, from the pool, before lowering himself underwater calmly - reverently. The CAMERA LINGERS on the surface before switching to Nico. While the first was violent, this is peaceful. The sunlight refracts through calm waters. His body is still, eyes open. Bubbles rise.

THEN - nothing.

CUT TO:

I/E. APARTMENT COMPLEX - CONTINUOUS

STILL SHOTS from the beginning, but backwards, as CREDITS are shown with each shot: The immaculate, gleaming POOL, perfectly still. The ROOFS OF BUILDINGS, trailing SMOKE. An OLD MAN, sitting on a LOUNGE CHAIR and smoking a CIGARETTE. And finally, AN EMPTY CHURCH in the middle of the day, CROSS starkly visible.

CUT TO BLACK.

END.
