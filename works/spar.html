Spar (Block C)

written by

Wyatt Newbill

A BLACK SCREEN. Heavy breathing and the sound of punches being thrown and blocked. A heavy THUMP.

INT. SPARRING RING (BLACK BOX) - DAY

A BODY hits the mat. Two boys are SPARRING, and ANTHONY (17) has just knocked IAN (17) to the ground. He raises his arms in triumph above Ian.

ANTHONY

That's what I'm talkin about, baby!

Ian rolls over on the ground, groaning.

IAN

Lucky shot.

Anthony just smirks at him.

ANTHONY

Yeah, keep telling yourself that.

He leans down and gives Ian a hand up, grunting. They tap GLOVES, then their hands are up and ready to spar.

Anthony moves in with a punch, but Ian evades it and responds with an uppercut. Anthony eats the punch, stumbling back. He touches his jaw gingerly.

ANTHONY (CONT'D)

(groaning)

The fuck!

IAN

You gonna fight, or bitch?

Anthony smolders. His hands tighten, breathing quickens. He suddenly surges forwards, swinging at Ian wildly. Ian tenses, ready to avoid the blow, when suddenly - a CLANG.

Ian reflexively turns towards the sound. Anthony's eyes widen and his hand slows slightly. Yet his shoulder keeps rotating - the punch lands awkwardly - but it's not on Ian's face. It's just below the ear. High on the neck.

CRACK.

Ian's legs fold. Anthony's victorious grin disappears.

ANTHONY

(hurriedly)

Hey, you good?

Ian's staring, shocked, at the ceiling. He locks terrified eyes with Anthony.

IAN

(whispering)

I think... I can't feel my legs.

(beat)

I can't... I can't feel my arms - why can't I feel my arms -

Anthony looks on in shock.

ANTHONY

(sinking to his knees)

Shit... shit-shit-shit-shit -

(fumbling for his phone)

I'm calling 9-1-1, alright? It'll be alright, Ian. It'll be alright - It'll be alright...

FADE OUT. TITLE CARD SHOWN.

INT. OUTSIDE ROOM (SINGLE DORM ROOM) - DAY

Anthony stands outside the door to Ian's room, hand poised to knock. He shifts nervously, wondering if this was the right decision. He closes his eyes, balls his hands up tight.

KNOCK.

INT. ROOM (SINGLE DORM ROOM) - CONTINUOUS

A CARETAKER opens the door to Ian's room. Anthony stares inside.

It is at first glance a normal teenager's room: POSTERS on the wall, a messy DESK, a CHESSBOARD, a PHOTO of Ian and Anthony.

Then the things that don't belong: STRETCHING BANDS. MEDICATION on the the nightstand. An appointment CALENDAR on the wall. And beside the door - a WHEELCHAIR.

Ian watches him from his BED, noticeably a little more unkempt. Anthony locks eyes with him nervously.

IAN

Hey.

Anthony opens his mouth, but nothing comes out.

CARETAKER

(stepping out)

I'll give you guys some privacy.

The caretaker walks out, and as he does, Anthony walks in, moving slightly around the wheelchair. He sits down in a CHAIR next to the bed. The door shuts.

IAN

Thought you'd never come.

ANTHONY

I -

(almost ashamedly)

You asked.

Ian scoffs a little at this, and Anthony looks up hurriedly.

ANTHONY (CONT'D)

I've been thinking a lot -

IAN

Don't.

Anthony pauses.

IAN (CONT'D)

Don't start with that.

ANTHONY

Huh?

IAN

The speech. Just don't.

They lapse into an awkward silence.

ANTHONY

How's... uh -

(gesturing vaguely around the room)

How's everything?

Ian looks at him.

ANTHONY (CONT'D)

Yeah, I dunno. Stupid question.

IAN

Ya think?

They both laugh. Anthony grows sober.

ANTHONY

I think about it every day, y'know.

IAN

It's been two months.

ANTHONY

(ashamedly)

I know.

IAN

Sometimes at night I close my eyes and I feel it happen again. Sometimes I dream I can move again.

ANTHONY

(softly, childlike)

Do you hate me?

IAN

...Sometimes.

Anthony looks up.

IAN (CONT'D)

Sometimes I hated myself. The world. Most of the time, I missed you.

(softly)

I miss you, man.

Silence.

ANTHONY

I didn't know what to say.

Ian scoffs and laughs bitterly. Anthony sighs.

ANTHONY (CONT'D)

Every time I came here... I'd sit in my car. Get to the front door - and I'd leave.

(pause)

Do - do you think we ever...

IAN

I didn't ask you here so you could apologize, man.

(beat)

I mean, everybody keeps acting like that. They keep talking like my life ended, y'know?

(beat)

It didn't. It just - changed.

ANTHONY

(pleading)

I just - I just don't know how to live with this. I don't know how to be us again.

IAN

It's different, now, man. We're different.

(beat.)

Y'know that thing your dad always says, about chess?

Anthony smiles a little and nods.

IAN (CONT'D)

He's right - you can't wish your last move back.

(softly)

You play the board you've got.

The caretaker knocks. Ian looks up.

IAN (CONT'D)

Hey, I - I gotta go. Getting fitted for a new wheelchair today.

ANTHONY

Need a push?

He begins to stand, but Ian waves him down.

IAN

It's alright - they've got it.

He gestures to the caretaker, who's walked in. Anthony sits back down, almost with an air of finality. The caretaker gently lifts Ian into the wheelchair.

Anthony watches. The room is silent. Ian catches Anthony's eye and offers a small smile. Anthony manages one back.

But as Ian is wheeled away and out the door, the smile drops from Anthony's face. He swallows as the realization of what he did sinks in. He did this.

Anthony stares ahead, unmoving, as his breathing grows heavier. Then steadier. Then heavier again.

CUT TO BLACK. END.
